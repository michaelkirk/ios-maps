Hills! Bike and walking routes now point out their steep climbs, and descents steep enough to make you squeeze the brakes, on the map and on the elevation chart.

Different strokes for different folks - walking and biking now show faster vs. comfier (lower traffic, typically safer) routes.

I gave the app a fresh coat of paint — new colors and type, a travel mode picker with icons, and a GO button you can actually read. Let me know if anything looks off, especially on a smaller phone.

Also new and worth testing if you're feeling virtuous:

- Transit lines show in their own colors.
- Locate Me zooms in closer.
- A spinner while routes load.
- An Attributions screen in Settings, to thank the open data and open source projects this app is built on.

Fixes:
- The trip controls no longer jump down the screen when directions can't be found.
- The last trip in a long list was hard to scroll to.
